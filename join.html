Undergraduate and Master's Projects

Research projects for undergraduate and master's students are available. If you are a Rice student and interested in working in the lab, send an email with your CV and transcripts.

  • Good performance in core robotics courses, such as COMP 462/562, COMP 450/550, or equivalent, is required to start a project.
  • The minimum time commitment is 6 months, to ensure high-quality projects.
