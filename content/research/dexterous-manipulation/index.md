Reorienting and repositioning an object inside a grasp is still one of the hardest problems in manipulation. We attack it from both ends: novel hardware such as wearable active-surface rings that move objects without lifting a finger, and dexterous policies that survive the jump from simulation to a real robot without fine-tuning.
