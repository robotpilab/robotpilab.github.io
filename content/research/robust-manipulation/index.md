Real-world manipulation is uncertain: models are approximate, perception is noisy, and contact is hard to predict. We design manipulation funnels, caging strategies, and compliant contact-rich behaviors that absorb that uncertainty instead of trying to eliminate it, so a robot can act reliably without precise models or real-time feedback.
